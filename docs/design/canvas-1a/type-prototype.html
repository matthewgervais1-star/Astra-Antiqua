THE ARGUMENT

Canvas 1a keeps Cinzel and then names a cost the brief never considered: a Roman inscriptional cap sets the titles for four cultures that are not Roman. "THE COUNCIL OF EL" in Trajanic caps is a small act of imperial typesetting, and the product's claim is that five theologies read the same sky on equal terms. The proposed fix is a code change rather than a font purchase: display drops to Spectral small caps on Egypt, Greece, Canaan and Carthage, and Cinzel keeps Rome and the app shell.

PROOF THE SMALL CAPS ARE REAL, NOT SYNTHESISED

Spectral SC carries its small capitals on the lowercase codepoints. Measured at 100px: in Spectral SC H is 81px wide and h is 74px, near cap width. In regular Spectral h is 58.2px, a true lowercase. So the glyphs below are drawn small capitals, and none of this relies on the browser scaling capitals down.
