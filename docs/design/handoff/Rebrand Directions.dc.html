HOW THE SKY IS COMPUTED

THE METHOD

THE EPHEMERIS

Positions come from the Swiss Ephemeris. They are geocentric ecliptic longitudes, computed for your place and hour. Nothing is estimated. Nothing is randomized.

HOUSES

Whole-sign houses, the oldest system on record. Your rising sign is your first house. Every house is exactly one sign.

ORBS

An aspect counts only inside these distances. When nothing is in orb, we say the day is quiet. We do not invent weather.

☌ 8°☍ 8°□ 7°△ 7°⋆ 5°TIGHTER = LOUDER
THE FIVE CULTURES

Every culture reads the same computed sky. A placement keeps its core meaning everywhere. What changes is the theology it must pass through, and that change is real: Egypt weighs, Greece questions, Canaan convenes, Carthage audits, Rome rules.

THE THREE RECEIVED BODIES

No ancient culture knew Uranus, Neptune, or Pluto. We admit them the way Rome admitted foreign gods: formally, and under native names. The receptions are modern work, made in the ancient manner. This page will always say so.

Everything works without an account. Your culture, charts, and feedback live on this device. An account adds sync and nothing else. Striking the account deletes the server copy.

Sextilis, under the Lion

The civic noon of the year: harvest brought in, accounts opened for inspection.
What is changing

Sol crosses the Lion attended by Iuppiter, and the month the old calendar called Sextilis opens its granaries. What was sown on credit in spring asks now to be counted; the Vulcanalia waits at the month’s end against fire in the dry fields.

Where it begins

The season entered at the Lion’s first degree on 23 July. Its center falls near the Ides, when Sol and Iuppiter stand closest and the temples of the forum keep their founding days.

What it touches

Standing, reputation, the ledger of favors. With Saturnus of the Sowing newly in the Ram and retrograde, obligations taken lightly in spring return for formal review; Venus crossed by Saturnus taxes a new affection with an old vow.

What it leaves behind

The postures of early summer: plans kept private, credit deferred, the pleasant vagueness of things not yet weighed.

The instruction

Bring the harvest indoors and count it before witnesses.

WHAT IS CHANGING

Semes rides highest, and the sea lanes are open: the city’s wealth is on the water. What was fitted out in spring is now at sail, and the season judges cargo, not intention.

WHERE IT BEGINS

It opened when the Lion received the sun, twenty days past. Its middle is now, at the height of the crossing weather, with Baal Hammon in his bounty beside the lamp.

WHAT IT TOUCHES

Ventures held with others, sworn partnerships, the harbor ledger. The elder Baal Hammon, withdrawn into the Ram, calls old vows in for audit; Astarte answers for the new ones.

WHAT IT LEAVES BEHIND

The fiction that a promise unwitnessed is no promise.

THE EGYPTIAN
DOORWAY

Egypt reads the sky as the river reads its banks: by return. Ma’at is the order that holds; Isfet is the incursion that must be balanced; the barque of Ra crosses the night and rises justified. To read in this voice is to stand in the Hall of Ma’at, where each placement is weighed like a heart against a feather.

Five voices read one computed sky, and the placement keeps its meaning in all of them. This voice renders it as balance kept or balance owed, in the vocabulary of flood, granary, offering, and return.
