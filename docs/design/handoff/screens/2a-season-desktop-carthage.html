THE SEASON · XXIII JULY — XXII AUGUST

THE SEASON OF
THE HIGH SEA

DAY 20 OF 31 · MIDPOINT PASSED
WHAT IS CHANGING

Semes rides highest, and the sea lanes are open. The city’s wealth is on the water. What you fitted out in spring is now at sail. The season judges cargo, not intention.

WHERE IT BEGINS

It opened when the Lion received the sun, twenty days past. Its middle is now, at the height of the crossing weather. Baal Hammon in his bounty stands beside the lamp.

WHAT IT TOUCHES

It touches ventures held with others, sworn partnerships, and the harbor ledger. The elder Baal Hammon has withdrawn into the Ram. He calls old vows in for audit. Astarte answers for the new ones.

WHAT IT LEAVES BEHIND

The fiction that a promise unwitnessed is no promise.
