THE FIVE VOICES · IV OF V

The Carthaginian Voice

◆ ◆ ◆

Carthage read the sky as a court of weights. Tanit, face of Baal, presides with Baal Hammon over a city of ledgers and harbors, and its decrees issue in the somber register of the Tophet, the precinct of vows, where what is promised is owed in full. Five voices read one sky in this almanac; this one answers with contracts.

The same placement keeps the same meaning in every voice. What changes here is the theology it must pass through: a harmony is a favorable weighing, a conflict is a vow called in, a conjunction is two powers sharing one altar.
