THE SEASON · XXIII JULY — XXII AUGUST · THE CARTHAGINIAN VOICE

THE SEASON OF
THE HIGH SEA

WHAT IS CHANGING

Semes rides highest, and the sea lanes are open: the city’s wealth is on the water. What was fitted out in spring is now at sail, and the season judges cargo, not intention.

WHERE IT BEGINS

It opened when the Lion received the sun, twenty days past. Its middle is now, at the height of the crossing weather, with Baal Hammon in his bounty beside the lamp.

WHAT IT TOUCHES

Ventures held with others, sworn partnerships, the harbor ledger. The elder Baal Hammon, withdrawn into the Ram, calls old vows in for audit; Astarte answers for the new ones.

WHAT IT LEAVES BEHIND

The fiction that a promise unwitnessed is no promise.
