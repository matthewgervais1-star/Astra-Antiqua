VOICE 05 · ROME

The state reads the sky the way it reads omens: formally.

Rome’s voice is civic. Auspices are taken, recorded, and acted on; the gods are parties to contracts, and a placement is a ruling entered into the public record. Where Egypt hears the river and Carthage hears the scale, Rome hears the magistrate.

Same sky, same core meaning in every voice. This voice renders it as law: a harmony is auspicious and may proceed, a conflict is an objection with standing, a conjunction is two powers seated in one temple.

TODAY, IN THIS VOICE · SPECIMEN

“Sol holds the Lion and Iuppiter Optimus Maximus stands beside him; the auspices are taken on the right. Venus is crossed by Saturnus of the Sowing: an old vow stands in the road of a new affection. Discharge the vow.”
