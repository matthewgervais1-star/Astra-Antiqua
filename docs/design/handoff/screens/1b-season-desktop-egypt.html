Akhet · the Inundation

23 JUL — 22 AUG 2026 · DAY 20 / 31
WHAT IS CHANGING

The river is up. Ra crosses the Lion with Amun beside him, and the flood covers the fields: the year’s work becomes visible all at once, yours included.

WHERE IT BEGINS

It opened on 23 July, when Sopdet rose before the sun and the inundation was declared. Its middle falls this week, with Ra and Amun at their closest.

WHAT IT TOUCHES

Public standing, stored grain, anything you have promised in writing. Geb, walking backward in the Ram, keeps the ground slow under new plans; build on what is already dry.

WHAT IT LEAVES BEHIND

The dry months’ habit of hoarding effort. When the water stands on the fields, holding back is only waste.
