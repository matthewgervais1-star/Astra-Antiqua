The auspices are taken on the right. What was sown in spring asks now to be counted, and the counting is itself the offering: office, name, the granary opened in sight of the forum.

Venus is crossed by Saturnus of the Sowing. An old vow stands in the road of a new affection, and the gods of Rome are patient with delay, merciless with pretense. Discharge the vow, or unsay it before the altar.
