THE EGYPTIAN
DOORWAY

Egypt reads the sky as the river reads its banks: by return. Ma’at is the order that holds; Isfet is the incursion that must be balanced; the barque of Ra crosses the night and rises justified. To read in this voice is to stand in the Hall of Ma’at, where each placement is weighed like a heart against a feather.

Five voices read one computed sky, and the placement keeps its meaning in all of them. This voice renders it as balance kept or balance owed, in the vocabulary of flood, granary, offering, and return.
