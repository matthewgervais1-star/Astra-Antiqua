WHERE THIS RELIGION WENT

This council did not vanish. A god from the southern deserts, Yahweh, was drawn into it and then identified with El himself. He took El’s titles, his tent, and his seat at the head of the assembly. In the oldest reading of Deuteronomy, he still stands among the sons of El, receiving Israel as his portion. Later, the rest of the council was demoted to angels, and the assembly became a heaven with one throne. The scale, the vow, and the court you read here are the older furniture of that house.
