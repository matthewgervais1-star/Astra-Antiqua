THE HALL OF MA’AT · YOUR STANDING PLACEMENT

YOUR RA IN
THE FISHES

☉ 13°22′ PISCES · FIFTH HOUSE · FIXED AT YOUR BIRTH
WHAT IT IS

Your Ra sails the waters before the world was made. His light reaches you through depth, not through noon. You were built to shine into hidden places. Most people around you shine at the surface.

HOW IT SHOWS

It sits in your fifth house, the house of the firstborn and the feast. Your making is private, and your play is serious. You finish things alone that others would workshop in public. The work arrives complete, like a barque out of the dark.

WHAT TO KEEP IN VIEW

Depth reads as absence to people who love you. Surface once a season, on purpose. Show one unfinished thing to one person. That is the offering this placement asks.
