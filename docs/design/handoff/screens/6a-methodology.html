HOW THE SKY IS COMPUTED

THE METHOD

THE EPHEMERIS

Positions come from the Swiss Ephemeris. They are geocentric ecliptic longitudes, computed for your place and hour. Nothing is estimated. Nothing is randomized.

HOUSES

Whole-sign houses, the oldest system on record. Your rising sign is your first house. Every house is exactly one sign.

ORBS

An aspect counts only inside these distances. When nothing is in orb, we say the day is quiet. We do not invent weather.

THE FIVE CULTURES

Every culture reads the same computed sky. A placement keeps its core meaning everywhere. What changes is the theology it must pass through, and that change is real: Egypt weighs, Greece questions, Canaan convenes, Carthage audits, Rome rules.

THE THREE RECEIVED BODIES

No ancient culture knew Uranus, Neptune, or Pluto. We admit them the way Rome admitted foreign gods: formally, and under native names. The receptions are modern work, made in the ancient manner. This page will always say so.
