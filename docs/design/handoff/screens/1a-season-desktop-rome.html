THE SEASON · READ IN THE ROMAN VOICE · 23 JULY – 22 AUGUST 2026

Sextilis, under the Lion

The civic noon of the year: harvest brought in, accounts opened for inspection.
What is changing

Sol crosses the Lion attended by Iuppiter, and the month the old calendar called Sextilis opens its granaries. What was sown on credit in spring asks now to be counted; the Vulcanalia waits at the month’s end against fire in the dry fields.

Where it begins

The season entered at the Lion’s first degree on 23 July. Its center falls near the Ides, when Sol and Iuppiter stand closest and the temples of the forum keep their founding days.

What it touches

Standing, reputation, the ledger of favors. With Saturnus of the Sowing newly in the Ram and retrograde, obligations taken lightly in spring return for formal review; Venus crossed by Saturnus taxes a new affection with an old vow.

What it leaves behind

The postures of early summer: plans kept private, credit deferred, the pleasant vagueness of things not yet weighed.

The instruction

Bring the harvest indoors and count it before witnesses.
