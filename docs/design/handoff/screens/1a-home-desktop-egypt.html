The Day’s Decree

In the Hall of Ma’at · read for the ninth day of the second month of Akhet

Ra stands conjoined with Amun in the Lion while the river rises. Increase of the visible kind is promised: office, name, the granary filled in sight of the town. Such light casts exact shadows, and what has been inflated will be measured against what is stored.

Isis crosses Geb, who is newly entered into the Ram and walking backward. What the mother would carry forward, the earth withholds. This is not Isfet; it is the field refusing seed before the water has stood its term.

Instruction: make the offering you have postponed, and let the record of it be exact.
